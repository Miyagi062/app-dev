# MY FAVOURITE SERIES: Steins Gate

**Steins Gate** is my favourite series out of all anime i'd say. I love how they made the series seems peaceful with some little bit of mystery in it. At first 6 or 7 episode, you would'nt even think that it is going to turn on a tragic road about a guy who just wanted to save his friends.

The story is like this; Okabe, a self-proclaimed *"mad scientist"* wanted to make a time-machine. But people thought he was just a lunatic and has screw-loose about this, since Time Travelling is impossible, not even an argument could save it. But then, he and his friends find a way to send a message to the past by using a modified microwave. 

`The discovery led them into a complex web of time travelling. After that, they had a conflict with SERN, an organization that wanted to seize their technology to earn this kind of power. This led to his friends dying over and over again through different timelines. Okabe, who had a power over his technology used it several times to save his friends, but to no avail; he cannot. But the story does not end there.`
